About Me

My background, technical interests, and the engineer I am working to become.

My Background

I'm a computer science learner from Egypt with a strong interest in Linux system administration and DevOps. My goal is to build a solid technical foundation and develop the skills needed to manage and troubleshoot real-world systems.

My Technical Interests

I enjoy working with Linux, understanding networking, writing Bash scripts, and learning how applications run on servers. I'm also exploring Docker, Git, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud infrastructure.

My Engineering Approach

I believe that understanding a problem is more important than applying a quick fix. When investigating an issue, I want to examine the available evidence, identify what is known and unknown, and choose the next investigation step based on what the evidence shows.

I use hands-on labs and projects to test my understanding, learn from mistakes, and improve my troubleshooting skills.

My Career Goal

My immediate goal is to grow into a capable Linux system administrator. Over time, I want to build on that foundation to work with automation, cloud infrastructure, and DevOps engineering.
